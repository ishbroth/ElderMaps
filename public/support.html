SUPPORT

ElderMaps, an unofficial fan project. Answers to the common questions, and how to reach us.
Need a hand? Write to support@eldermaps.com. Tell us what device you use (iPhone, Android or the website), what you tried, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Getting started

How do I make an account?

Press ENTER on the map, then CONSCRIPT. Type your email and we send you a link; open it on the same device. You then choose a name, a four-digit serf number (your password), your character and the sigil of your city.

How do I sign in again?

Press ENTER, type your email and serf number, and press ENTER THE REALM. After five wrong tries the account locks: write to us and we will unlock it.

The sign-in email did not arrive.

Check the spam folder, and wait a minute before asking again. If it still does not come, write to us at the address above.

I forgot my serf number.

Write to us at the address above from the email your account uses, and we will help you back in.

The map

What do the three looks mean?

The button at the top of the map cycles between the northern look, the vivid look (a relief map with real clouds, which you can switch on and off) and the parchment look.

How do I find places?

Turn on ME and walk: a place within about 500 metres is discovered. Places you have found are drawn solid; places you have not are drawn as outlines. You can also search for a city, a keep or a waterfall at the top.

Are the map and directions free?

Yes. Each device can open the map a limited number of times a day, and use Google's routing a limited number of times a day (after which a free open router is used).

What is the road's voice?

The ♪ button turns the spoken turn-by-turn directions on and off while you navigate.

Glimpses, Town Legends and combat

What is a glimpse?

A picture of a place, re-imagined in the style of its land, made from a photograph of the real place. A glimpse costs 100 septims (400 for a great city). Once made it is shared with everyone who unlocks it, and the first person to pay is shown in the "Discovered by" line.

What is a Town Legend?

A street-level map of a city with every place you have glimpsed marked on it. It is bought once per city, for 4,800 septims.

How does combat work?

After a glimpse you can clear the place. Hold to charge and release to strike; tap for a quick blow. Potions heal, shouts slow time, and clearing a place earns loot, which you collect by visiting it in person.

What do I do with loot?

Open the Inventory tab: the forge and alchemist turn loot into weapons, potions and shouts. Loot also builds rooms and furniture in your house.

Septims and purchases

How do I buy septims?

In the Inventory tab, press BUY SEPTIMS. Packs of 400, 1,800 and 4,400 septims are paid for on Stripe's secure page (Apple Pay and Google Pay appear there when available). The septims arrive a moment after paying.

I paid and the septims did not arrive.

Wait a minute and reopen the Inventory tab. If they are still missing, write to us with your account email and the time of the payment.

Can I get a refund?

Write to us at the address above with your account email and what happened, and we will look at it. Septims already spent on a glimpse or Town Legend cannot be returned.

Your account

How do I change my sigil or character?

In the Inventory tab, press the quill beside your name.

How do I delete my account?

In the Inventory tab, choose Delete my account at the bottom, type DELETE and your serf number, and confirm. Everything is removed permanently. See the privacy policy.
