PRIVACY POLICY

ElderMaps, an unofficial fan project. Last updated 24 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you conscript (sign up). You sign in with your email and the four-digit serf number you chose (that number is your password), or with a sign-in link we email you.
  • Your character: the name, type, sex and city sigil you choose.
  • Your location, only if you allow it and only while the app or page is open: to show where you stand, to discover places as you reach them, and to give directions. The server checks your position against the place when a discovery is recorded; it is not kept as a track of where you have been.
  • What you do in the game: the places you have found and cleared, the pictures you have unlocked, your septims, loot, potions and weapons, and your house. These are tied to your account so they follow you to other devices.
  • Purchases: what you bought and your septim balance. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe (on the website); we never see or store them.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase stores your account and game data.
  • Google provides the maps, place search, directions and Street View. Searches you make are sent to Google.
  • Stripe processes payments on the website.
  • Google's image model (Gemini) creates the pictures of places from photographs (including, as a last resort, Street View images, which are used once and not kept).
  • Resend delivers the sign-in emails.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites. There are no advertising or analytics trackers.

Pictures of places

A picture made of a place is shared with everyone who unlocks that place. Your name is never put on it other than the "Discovered by" line, which shows the character name of the first person to pay for it (and follows that name if you change it).

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time: open the Inventory tab, choose Delete my account, type DELETE, enter your serf number and confirm. This permanently removes your account, character, septims, loot, weapons, house and found places. Pictures already made of places stay, without anything linking them to you.

Children

ElderMaps is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: support@eldermaps.com. Support page.
